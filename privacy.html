Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

NFA Wishlist (“NFA”, “we”) is a free Chrome extension and website for saving gifts from independent shops and sharing them with friends and family. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it. The short version: we collect only what's needed to keep your list working, we don't sell or share it with advertisers, and we don't track you around the web.

What we collect

When you save things to your list (extension)

  • The items you save: the product's name, description, price and currency, whether it's in stock, the store's name, the product page address, and the address of its photo. The extension reads these from the page only when you choose “Save to NFA Wishlist” (or tap the save button). Anything you type, like a new name, description or price, is saved too.
  • Your list: its name, the order of your items, and whether you marked something “I own this”.
  • Items you add yourself on the website (“Add item”), and a photo, if you upload one. Photos are resized in your browser before upload and stored with Supabase. Like product photos from shops, anyone with the photo's address can view it, so don't upload anything private.
  • A note and shipping address, if you add them (both optional), and who you chose to show each one to: anyone with your link, anyone who signs in with Google, or nobody. We only send them to the people you chose. Clearing them deletes them.
  • An anonymous account ID: the first time you save, we create an account for you with no name, email or password. It's a random ID that lets your list sync and keeps others from changing it.
  • Link codes: when you share, we create a random code for your list's link, and a separate private code for your own edit link.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

  • Signing in is never required. If you choose “Sign in with Google” in the extension or on the website, Google tells us your email address, name and profile photo address, and we link them to your account so you can open your list on other computers and edit it on the website. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • On the website, your sign-in is kept in your browser's localStorage (not a cookie) until you sign out.
  • If you've used NFA with the same Google account on another computer, we offer to copy this browser's items into that list. Your earlier list stays as it was.

We don't read your browsing history, and the extension can't see any page unless you ask it to save from that page. See “Extension permissions” below.

When friends and family view your list (website)

  • Purchase marks: when a friend or family member taps “Mark as gifted”, we store that the item was marked, with no name. Their browser keeps a private code so only that browser can undo it.
  • Friends and family don't need to sign in. If one of them chooses to sign in with Google, we receive the same details described above.
  • Small notes in their own browser (localStorage, not cookies): which items they marked, the last store page they opened from the list (so we can ask “Did you buy it?” when they come back; forgotten after 3 days), and which lists' edit links were opened in that browser (so list owners don't see purchases).

The list owner never sees what's been marked as gifted, or by whom.

Automatically

  • Like any website, our hosting providers receive your IP address, browser type and the page requested when you load a page or the extension syncs. They use this to deliver the service and protect it from attacks.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, and we don't set cookies.

How we use it

Only to run NFA: to save and sync your list, show it to the people you share it with, keep purchases hidden from you, and keep the service secure. We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores lists, items, purchase marks and Google sign-in details (servers in the United States).
  • Google handles “Sign in with Google”, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website nfawishlist.com.
  • GitHub hosts an older copy of the website (swirl2001.github.io/nfa) during our move to nfawishlist.com.
  • Online shops: list pages show product photos loaded straight from the shop's website, so that shop can see a request from your browser. We tell browsers not to send which page you came from. When you open a product, you go to the shop's own site, under its own privacy policy.

Who can see your list

Anyone who has your list's link can view it. The link contains a random code and isn't listed anywhere, and we ask search engines not to index list pages. Only share it with people you trust, and keep your private edit link to yourself: anyone with it can change your list.

Extension permissions

  • Context menus: adds “Save to NFA Wishlist” when you right-click.
  • Active tab and scripting: reads the product details from the page you're on, only at the moment you choose to save.
  • Storage: keeps a copy of your list on your computer so it opens instantly.
  • Side panel: shows your list beside the page.
  • Identity: opens Google's sign-in window, only when you choose “Sign in with Google”.
  • Access to all sites (optional, off by default): only if you turn on the floating save button. It lets the button appear on product pages. Turning the button off removes this access.

Affiliate links

Today, NFA has no affiliate links. The extension never adds, changes or removes affiliate links or cookies, and it never will. If we add affiliate links in the future, they'll appear only on the website's list pages behind a button a friend or family member chooses to tap, with a clear note that we may earn a commission, and 10% of every commission will go to SF community groups. We'll update this policy before that happens.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your list until you delete it. Deleting an item removes it from our servers.
  • Signing out removes your sign-in from that browser or the extension. Your list stays in your account.
  • To delete your whole list and account (including Google sign-in details), email us (below) with your list's link and we'll delete everything within 30 days. Uninstalling the extension removes the copy on your computer, but not the list on our servers.
  • Friends and family can clear their purchase codes and notes by clearing site data for nfawishlist.com in their browser.

Children

NFA isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll say so in the extension.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: hello@nfawishlist.com. NFA Wishlist is made in San Francisco, California.
