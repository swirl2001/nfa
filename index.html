Wishlists for things worth giving.
Not Amazon.

NFA Wishlist is a free Chrome extension. Save gifts from any independent shop, share one link with friends and family, and they can quietly mark what they've bought so nobody doubles up. The surprise stays a surprise.

Chrome extension coming soon

How it works

  1. Save. Right-click any product photo on a shop's site and choose “Save to NFA Wishlist”, or tap the (+) NFA button if you've turned it on.
  2. Share. Send your list's link to friends and family. They don't need an account or the extension.
  3. Gift. Friends tap “Mark as purchased” so nobody buys the same thing twice. You never see what's been bought.

No Amazon, ever

NFA stands for “Not F***ing Amazon.” We never link to Amazon or its stores, never load their images, and never send them traffic. If you save something from Amazon, we keep its name and price and point you to independent shops instead.

Shop small, shop local

NFA is made in San Francisco to send gift money to independent makers and neighborhood shops. It's free. If we ever earn commissions from shop links, 10% of every commission will go to SF community groups.
