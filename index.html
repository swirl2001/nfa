WISHLIST -
Shop small, Shop local

NFA Wishlist is a free Chrome extension. Save gifts from any independent shop. Share the link with friends and family. Gifters quietly mark what they've gifted so nobody doubles up. The surprise stays a surprise.

Add to Chrome

How it works

  1. Save items to your wishlist. Right-click any product photo on a shop's website. Choose “Save to NFA Wishlist”, or tap the (+) NFA button if you've turned it on.
  2. Share. Sign in once, then send your wishlist's link to friends and family. They don't need an account or the extension.
  3. Gift. Gifters tap “Mark as gifted” so nobody gifts a duplicate gift. Recipients don't see what's been bought, unless they choose to peek.

NOT F-ING @MA20N, ever

NFA supports an @MA20N-free lifestyle. We never link to @MA20N or its stores. We never load their images, and we never send them traffic. If you save something from @MA20N, we provide a search function to locate the item at independent shops instead.

Why I made this

We try to be a no-@MA20N household. That's harder than it sounds when birthdays and holidays come around. Every wishlist seems to lead back to @MA20N. I wanted a wishlist that didn’t spoil surprises or result in duplicates that also supports my values. So I built one.

We’re adults, we can pay for shipping

This isn't anti-shopping. We're pro-gifting. But some people already have enough money for a hundred lifetimes, so we'd rather spread the spending around: to makers, small brands, and the shop down the street.

Free, and honest about money

NFA Wishlist is free, with every feature. Someday I'd like it to earn a little from shop links (affiliate links). When it does, 10% of every commission will go to local community groups. To be honest, I'm still figuring out how all of that works. Today there are no affiliate links at all.

I hope it's useful, and I hope it makes this holiday season a little easier. 🙂

Sean
